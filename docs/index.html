How to use

  1. Export the Tally purchase register and sales register to .xlsx or .csv.
  2. Download GSTR-2A, GSTR-2B and GSTR-1 files from the GST portal, then upload each in Import.
  3. Upload the Tally purchase register as Books - Purchase Register and the sales register as Books - Sales Register.
  4. Review and correct the column mapping before importing.
  5. Open Reconcile, choose a comparison, and run it.
  6. Export the result to Excel and review exceptions before taking any ITC, tax, or filing decision.
  7. In 3B, enter the portal and books totals and export the comparison.
  8. Use the same GSTIN and period for all related files. Period format is MMYYYY, e.g. September 2026 = 092026.

Important limitations

  • It does not connect to a GSP or download returns automatically.
  • Portal export formats vary. Column mapping must be checked; multi-sheet or JSON exports may need format-specific handling.
  • GSTR-3B is summary-level and is compared by totals, not by invoice number.
  • Matching uses invoice number + party GSTIN. Credit/debit notes, amendments, reverse-charge, ISD, imports and duplicate invoice numbers need more specialised logic.
  • A match does not itself establish ITC eligibility or legal compliance.
  • Data is stored unencrypted in this browser profile. Use a trusted device and clear local data when done.
  • On a phone, use the browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen to keep it as an app icon.
